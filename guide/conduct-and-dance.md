---
title: Conduct and dance
---

Your whole body can lead the music. Open the [Conductor](tool:conductor) and the [Song](tool:song) tools to try these; each page there starts with what to do.

## Conduct a score

Load a MIDI or MusicXML score (a demo is built in), switch the conductor on and beat time in front of the camera. A sampled orchestra plays and follows your tempo and your loudness. You choose which parts play, and the orchestra downloads only when you switch it on.

:::details Hand or body
By default your beating hand's strokes are the beats. Choose **body** and several joints (arm and trunk) are read: start, hold, fermata and cut-off come from your body, and your other hand can shape loudness and staccato. *Learn my timing* measures how far your trunk lags a click, so the beat lands when you meant it. Body conducting needs most of your upper body in view.
:::

:::details Start, hold and stop with poses
A pose model you taught in [Your poses](tool:poses) can start, hold and cut off the conductor: raise your arm and keep still to get ready, then give the upbeat. See [Your poses](tool:poses) for how to teach "ready", "hold" and "cut off".
:::

## Dance the orchestra

Choose **Dance the orchestra** and the score follows a dancer instead of a conductor: the tempo follows your stride, a written fermata holds for one cycle of yours, and the piece loops while you keep dancing. Turn body tracking on in the editor's [Body](settings:Body) section first, or let the conductor ask for it.

## A song that follows you

In the [Song](tool:song) tool, load a song file. Its beat is tracked in your browser. Tick *Follow the dancer* and the song's tempo follows your pulse within a range you set (10 percent by default), whether you bounce on every beat or every other. *Start on your step* lands a beat of the song on your step when you press Play. Pausing the song keeps the lock.

:::details Conduct a recording
The song itself can follow your conducting: you choose which beat of the song your first beat lands on, and on a hold the song can fade and slow down instead of cutting. Both are off until you set them.
:::

## Conduct the chords

[Conduct the Chords](instrument:Conduct the Chords) plays the chords of a piece, voice-led and arpeggiated, on your beat.
