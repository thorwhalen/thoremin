---
title: Thoremin
---

Thoremin turns your camera into a musical instrument. Hold a hand up and it plays; make a face and it plays chords; mime a drum, a bass, a guitar or a flute and it plays those; conduct an orchestra or dance to a song. It runs in your browser: nothing you play is uploaded.

## Start in one minute

1. Open [the app](app:) in Chrome (other browsers work with a few gaps: see [troubleshooting](troubleshooting.html)) and allow the camera.
2. Tap anywhere to start the sound. Browsers need a tap before they will make any.
3. Hold a hand up. Move it left and right for pitch, up and down for loudness. Raise a second hand for a second voice.

No camera? Add `?slot.source=synthetic-hands` to the address and a pretend hand plays.

## Pick something to try

- **[Field instruments](area:field)**: play the air like a theremin, with fingers, faces and open hands shaping the sound.
- **[Air instruments](area:air)**: strike a drum, pluck a bass, strum a guitar, blow a flute, at nothing.
- **[Chords and harmony](area:chords)**: point at chords on a map or a wheel instead of notes.
- **[Sign instruments](area:sign)**: fingerspell and see or hear the letters.
- **Conduct and dance**: the [Conductor](tool:conductor) follows your beat; the [Song](tool:song) follows your pulse.
- **Teach it yourself**: [Your faces](tool:faces), [Your hand shapes](tool:hand-shapes) and [Your poses](tool:poses) learn the faces, shapes and poses you can make.

:::details Instruments, sounds and saving
An **instrument** is a named saved set of every setting: scales, sounds, ranges, finger routing, what the face does, what is drawn on the video. A **sound** is just the timbre (a bell, a pad, a reed). Open the instrument list to browse them: star the ones you like, give them your own tags, and hover one for a short summary. Editing an instrument marks it changed; **Save** overwrites it and **Save as new** keeps both. Your working state survives a reload.
:::
