---
seed: ASL Chords
area: sign
do:
  - Fingerspell the letters A to G.
  - Each letter plays the chord of the key whose root has that letter.
  - The chord's name shows under the sign.
settings: Sign language, Sound
---
Letters play chords: in D major, F plays F-sharp minor and C plays C-sharp diminished, because the key's accidentals apply.

:::details How it works
The recognised letter is turned into the chord of the key, then voiced like the face chords. The key, sound and voicing are the same settings as the face chords (**Face**), and they are also in the Sign language section.
:::
