---
seed: Sign Map
area: sign
do:
  - Fingerspell a letter from A to G with your higher hand: its chord lights up on the map.
  - Raise or lower your right hand to play a melody on that chord's notes.
  - The chord stays while you play; spell another letter to change it.
settings: Chord map, Sign language, Sound, Face
---
One hand chooses the chord by spelling it, the other plays over it. In D major, F lights F-sharp minor and C lights C-sharp diminished.

:::details How it works
The sign reader names your letter. A letter read for a fifth of a second lights its chord on the chord map, and the chord holds while your hand passes through other shapes. The melody hand's height picks a note from the chord, and tilting it changes the set (chord tones, pentatonic, the scale). Your right hand plays the melody; change that with Chord hand in [Chord map](settings:Chord map). The key is in **Sound**; how the chord sounds is in **Face**, once you set the face to *chord*.
:::
