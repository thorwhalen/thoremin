area: sign
do:
  - Allow the camera and hold a hand up.
  - Fingerspell a letter or sign a word.
  - The sign is written large on the video, with a bar for every sign it knows.
settings: Sign language
---
See what the camera reads: the American Sign Language alphabet and a few common words (yes, no, hello, thank you, please, sorry, bye).

:::details How it works
A small recogniser reads the shape of your hand. It downloads only when you first open an ASL instrument, so the rest of the app never pays for it.
:::
