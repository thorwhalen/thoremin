area: field
do:
  - Hold a hand up so all five fingers show.
  - Curl the index finger for brighter tone, the middle for vibrato.
  - Curl the ring finger to pan, the pinky to bend the pitch.
settings: Hand
---
Each finger is a control. Lines on the video show which finger drives which effect, with its value.

:::details How it works
Every finger can be routed to an effect (brightness, vibrato, pan, pitch bend, octave, gate), continuously or as a trigger, with its own sensitivity. This instrument routes the index to brightness, the middle to vibrato, the ring to pan and the pinky to pitch bend.
:::

:::details Change the routing
In the editor, **Hand** lists the five fingers; pick an effect for each and how strongly it reacts.
:::
