area: field
do:
  - Hold a hand up so all your fingers show.
  - Bring the index fingertip toward the thumb for a brighter tone, the middle for vibrato.
  - Bring the ring fingertip toward the thumb to pan, the pinky to bend the pitch.
settings: Hand
---
Each finger is a control, measured by how close its tip is to your thumb. Lines on the video show which finger drives which effect, with its value.

:::details How it works
Every finger can be routed to an effect (brightness, vibrato, pan, pitch bend, octave, gate), continuously or as a trigger, with its own sensitivity. This instrument routes the index to brightness, the middle to vibrato, the ring to pan and the pinky to pitch bend.
:::

:::details Change the routing
In the editor, open **Hand**, then **Finger effects**. It lists the four fingers: pick an effect for each, continuous or trigger, and how strongly it reacts.
:::
