---
seed: Everything
area: field
do:
  - Play notes with your wrist.
  - Curl the four fingers for brightness, vibrato, pan and an octave jump.
  - Smile and change your expression to change the timbre.
settings: Sound, Hand, Face
---
Everything at once: wrist notes, all four fingers routed, the open hand brightening the tone, and the face shaping the timbre.

:::details How it works
It is a maximal setting of the same controls the other field instruments use, so it is a good place to see how much one instrument can hold. Switch things off in **Hand** and **Face** until it plays the way you like.
:::
