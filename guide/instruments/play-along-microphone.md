---
seed: Play Along (microphone)
area: chords
do:
  - Allow the microphone, then play a guitar, a piano, or a recording.
  - The chords it hears light up on the map a moment later.
  - Play a melody over them with your hand.
settings: Chord map
---
The microphone chooses the chords; your hand plays the melody over them.

:::details How it works
The app listens for the notes sounding and matches them against the chords it knows. Only a clear chord counts, and only once it has lasted about a quarter of a second, so a passing note does not change the chord. The microphone is opened only while a map follows it, and the browser asks your permission first. Play the chords near the computer; a quiet room helps.
:::

:::details Settings
In **Chord map**: what chooses the chords (your hand, a MIDI keyboard, or the microphone), and how long a heard chord must last before it counts.
:::
