Conduct a piece and hear its harmony: the chords of the score, played on your beat.

:::details How it works
The piece is read for its chords, beat by beat, setting aside passing notes and drums. As you conduct, the chord of the moment is played voice-led and arpeggiated on your beat, while the conductor plays the first violin's melody; the quartet is not doubled. Where the score marks a pause, the chord holds as long as you hold it; when you speed up or slow down, the chords follow. This instrument needs the Conductor.
:::

:::details Settings
**Chord chart** in the editor: on or off, and how much of the piece is read as one chord (every beat for fast harmony, two beats or a bar for slow). How the chord sounds is the chord sound in **Sound**; the piece, the parts and the conducting are in **Conductor**.
:::
