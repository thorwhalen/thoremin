---
seed: Face Wheel
area: chords
do:
  - Look at the camera with a calm face.
  - Hold an expression for a moment: a smile moves to one chord, a sad face to another.
  - Each expression you hold is a move on the wheel.
settings: Chord wheel, Face
---
The chord wheel steered by your face instead of your hand.

:::details How it works
Each expression stands for one of the wheel's moves: for example a smile goes to the dominant and a sad face to the relative minor. Hold the expression for a short moment and the move is made, then relax before the next one. Only your face moves the wheel, so your hands make no sound here; the chord sounds as you change it. Set the wheel to steer with both if you also want to push it with a hand.
:::

:::details Settings
In **Chord wheel**: what steers it (your hand, your face, or both) and how long an expression must be held. The face model itself is set up in **Face**.
:::
