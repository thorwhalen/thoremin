---
seed: Pitch Bender
area: field
do:
  - Play a note by moving your hand.
  - Pinch the index finger toward the thumb to bend the pitch down.
  - Release to let it spring back.
settings: Hand
---
A whole-tone bend under your fingertips, like a guitar string.

:::details How it works
The distance between the index fingertip and the thumb is routed to pitch bend. A full pinch is a whole-tone bend.
:::
