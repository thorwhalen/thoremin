---
seed: Wrist Theremin
area: field
do:
  - Hold a hand up and move it: the wrist, not the fingertip, plays the note.
  - Make a fist to silence that hand.
  - Open the hand again to bring the sound back.
settings: Sound, Hand
---
Steadier and easier for a beginner than the fingertip: the whole hand is the pointer, and a closed fist mutes it.

:::details How it works
The note position comes from the wrist instead of the index fingertip, which jitters less. A closed hand is read as a fist and gates the voice off, an open hand lets it sound.
:::
