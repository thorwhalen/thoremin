  - Stand back so the camera sees you from the head to the hips; it loads a Haydn string quartet.
  - Open the Conductor tool and conduct with your whole arm, as on a podium.
  - The quartet's chords play on your beats; raise your arm and hold still to pause, drop it to stop.
settings: Chord chart, Conductor, Sound, Face

[Conduct the Chords](instrument:Conduct the Chords), conducted with your body instead of a hand held up to the camera: the body camera follows your beating arm and your trunk, so you can stand back and move freely.

:::details How it works
The body camera finds the beat in your beating arm and the bounce of your trunk. Raise the arm and hold still to get ready, give one upbeat, and the next stroke is the downbeat. Hold the arm up and still to pause, drop it to stop. Everything else works as in [Conduct the Chords](instrument:Conduct the Chords). It needs the Conductor and the body camera, so it starts a little slower than a hands-only instrument.
:::
