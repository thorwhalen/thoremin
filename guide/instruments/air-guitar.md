You decide what a chord shape looks like. It learns your own hand shapes, then plays the right chord when you strum.

:::details How it works
The chord shapes you enrol are the ones it recognises, so you can use any shapes you like. A strum is predicted before the frame that shows it. A hand-shape model you carved with [Your hand shapes](tool:hand-shapes) can be the chords.
:::
