---
seed: Pose Chords
area: field
do:
  - Stand back so the camera sees your whole body.
  - Hold a pose a moment: both arms up, the left arm out, the right arm out, or a crouch.
  - Each pose plays its own chord of the key; standing at rest plays none.
settings: Categorizer chords, Sound
---
Four poses, four chords. It plays a shipped example of a pose categorizer, made from the app's own scripted body, so it may not read you well: teach it your own poses in **Your poses** and choose them in [Categorizer chords](settings:Categorizer chords).

:::details How it works
The body camera reads your pose, and the nearest of the four poses the example learned, held a moment, chooses the chord. The first pose plays the key's home chord, then the fourth, the fifth and the sixth; each can be changed in **Categorizer chords**. It needs the body camera, so it starts a little slower than a hands-only instrument.
:::
