---
seed: DJ deck
area: daw
extension: daw
do:
  - Pick the DJ deck, then load a song in the Song tool and press Play.
  - Pinch with your right hand and raise or lower it to sweep the filter.
  - Pinch with your left hand to repeat the last beat; move it to make the repeat shorter or longer.
settings: DAW
---
Play DJ over a song. The song runs through a deck with a filter, an equalizer, an echo, a beat repeat, a key shift, a loop and a brake, and your hands work the two you reach for most: the filter and the beat repeat. Your own instrument plays on top, untouched by the effects.

:::details How it works
The deck keeps time with the song: it follows the song's tempo while it plays, and its own tempo when it is paused. The song's first beat is not always bar 1, so press **Bar 1 here** in the DAW section on a downbeat, and **Bar -1 beat** or **Bar +1 beat** to move the bar by one beat. A repeat starts on the next sixteenth note, so it lands in time even when your pinch is a little late.

Every effect has a control in the DAW section and a command in the palette (Ctrl+K or Cmd+K, then type "DAW"): filter, equalizer and its kills (cut the lows, mids or highs), echo and echo-out (the song stops on the next beat and the echo rings on), beat repeat, key shift (up to an octave either way, the tempo unchanged), loop (a quarter bar to 8 bars, halve and double), vinyl brake, **Lock** (keep the effects as they are and free your hands) and **Cancel all** (every effect off at once). Hands, in the same section, swaps the hands or frees them both.
:::

:::details Good to know
Play a starter loop from the Loop library in the DAW section with the song: it starts on the next bar, in time. The loops have their own commands in the palette: type "Loops:" (and "Looper:" for the loop tracks).

Pick the DJ deck before you load the song. If a song was already loaded, reload the page, then load it again: the DAW section says when the song is not going through the deck. Switching to another instrument resets the deck's bar, and the song keeps playing.
:::
