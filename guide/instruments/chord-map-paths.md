The chord map as a teacher: it draws a famous chord path ahead of you, tells you what each move does, and shows the seven chords on a turning torus.

:::details How it works
This is the Chord Map with three helpers switched on. The dashed trail is Pachelbel's ground (I V vi iii IV I IV V), drawn from the chord you are holding to the next ones; play the next chord and the trail moves on, wander off and it waits where you left it. The words on the moves around your chord say how many notes the move keeps, whether it turns darker or brighter, and how expected it is after the chord you hold. The torus shows why every chord is one reach away: on its surface each of the seven touches all the others, along three loops (seconds, thirds, fourths), the same three directions as the honeycomb.
:::

:::details Settings
**Chord map** in the editor: **Trail to follow** picks another path (the Romanesca, the circle of fifths, the Andalusian cadence, the fifties progression, and more) or none; **Trail length** how far ahead it shows; **Move captions** the words on the moves; **Show the chords on a turning torus** and its speed. The key is in **Sound**.
:::
