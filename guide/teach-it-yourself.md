---
title: Teach it yourself
---

Instead of fixed expressions, shapes and poses, you can show thoremin YOURS and it learns to tell them apart. One tool, three ways to use it:

- [Your faces](tool:faces): the faces you can make. Play chords with them, or fire commands.
- [Your hand shapes](tool:hand-shapes): the shapes of one hand. They can be the air guitar's chords, or fire commands.
- [Your poses](tool:poses): your body's poses. They can start, hold and stop the conductor.

## How it goes, in five steps

1. **Record.** Press *Record my faces* (or hand shapes, or poses). Stay relaxed for a few seconds while it learns your rest, then make your faces one after another.
2. **Sort.** Stop, and your holds are grouped. Pick how many groups, drag holds between them, name them. Undo and redo are there.
3. **Check.** Each group shows how clearly it differs from its neighbours, and says in plain words what to do when two are close.
4. **Try it.** *Trying* shows live what it reads. *Quiz me* names each group twice, in random order, and shows which you can find again.
5. **Use it.** Save it, then press *Play it*, choose it in the editor's [Face categorizer](settings:Face categorizer) or [Pose categorizer](settings:Pose categorizer) section, or bind it to commands in [Gestures](tool:gestures).

:::details Good takes
Use even light on your face ("Too dark" shows when the room is too dim). Make each face clearly different from the others and hold it for a moment. Teach "Neutral" (a relaxed face that should play nothing) so an untaught face is not mistaken for a group.
:::
