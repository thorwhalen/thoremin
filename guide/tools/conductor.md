Conduct a score with your hand or your body. A loaded MIDI or MusicXML score (or a demo) plays through a sampled orchestra that follows you; the orchestra loads only when you switch it on, and you choose which parts play.

:::details Hand, body or dancing
*Conduct with* your **hand** (the default; the strokes of your beating hand are the beats) or your **body** (start, hold, fermata and cut-off come from the body, and a second hand can shape loudness and staccato). *Dance the orchestra* makes the score follow a dancer's stride instead. *Learn my timing* measures how far your trunk lags a click.
:::

:::details Conduct the chords
The instrument [Conduct the Chords](instrument:Conduct the Chords) plays the chords of a piece on your beat.
:::
