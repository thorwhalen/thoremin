tool: annotations
title: Annotations
do:
  - Press Annotation mode at the bottom right.
  - While recording, tap a label (or press 1 to 9) to mark what is happening.
  - Export the labels from the Export panel.
---
Mark what was happening, when, so a recording can be cut into labelled segments afterwards.

:::details How it works
Name your annotations in the sheet. An *interval* has a start and an end, a *point* is an instant; group annotations so turning one on turns the others off, and give one a lead-in if you want its first moments trimmed. Open annotations blink and are burned into the video corner. Annotations are written next to the features, on the same clock. Export them as an Audacity label track, WebVTT, CSV, Praat TextGrid or OTIO.
:::
