tool: gestures
title: Gestures
do:
  - Open Tools, then Gestures.
  - Choose a command for a hand pose: fist, open palm or pinch.
  - Make the pose: the command runs, hands-free.
---
Run commands without touching the screen. Bind a hand pose, or any group you taught in [Your faces](tool:faces), [Your hand shapes](tool:hand-shapes) or [Your poses](tool:poses), to a command such as mute, octave up or load an instrument.

:::details Your categories
*Your categories* binds each group of a saved model to a command: entering the group fires the command. A model's movements (the gestures recorded in [Your hand shapes](tool:hand-shapes)) are listed under its groups: a movement runs its command once each time you make it. Just after you choose a model, or after the Trainer has hushed the instrument, the list says it is getting ready to listen: hold still for a moment, since a movement made before then is not heard. Commands that could lose your work (saving over an instrument) are not allowed here.
:::
