---
tool: poses
title: Your poses
do:
  - Open Tools, then Your poses, then Record my poses.
  - Do the three short demonstrations (closer and further, a step aside, a slight turn), then each pose, held for a moment.
  - Sort and name the groups. The conductor can use them to start, hold and stop.
settings: Pose categorizer
---
The same tool as [Your faces](tool:faces), for your body. It needs most of your body in view, otherwise it says "Cannot see you".

:::details Left and right
Tick *Left and right are the same pose* before you record if one arm up should be one pose, whichever arm.
:::

:::details Use them
A pose model can start, hold and cut off the [Conductor](tool:conductor). In the editor, the Pose categorizer section chooses which model an instrument plays.
:::
