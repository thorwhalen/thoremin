---
tool: hand-shapes
title: Your hand shapes
do:
  - Open Tools, then Your hand shapes, then Record my hand shapes.
  - Make your shapes one after another, as in Your faces.
  - Sort and name the groups, then bind them to commands or to the air guitar.
---
The same tool as [Your faces](tool:faces), for the shape of one hand. A shape is read relative to the hand, so left and right hands share it, or you can choose a hand.

:::details Where the shapes go
A saved shape model can be the air guitar's chords ([Air Guitar](instrument:Air Guitar)), or fire a command when you make a shape ([Gestures](tool:gestures)).
:::
