A guided take: it asks you to look left, smile, open your mouth and so on, and learns from what you do. [Your faces](tool:faces) is the newer, easier way to teach faces; the Trainer is where cues and routines live.

:::details Where takes go
In a browser with a folder picker (Chrome, Edge), press *Save takes to a folder...* under the Start button and every take is written straight into it. Safari and Firefox have no folder picker, and take a zip instead.
:::
