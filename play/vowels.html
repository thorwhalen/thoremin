Vowels

Touch the chart and hold to hear a vowel; slide to glide between them. Press Start and say a long vowel ("eee", "ooo", "aaa"): a dot shows where it sits. In Vowel Pop, hold the dot on a vowel's balloon until it bursts. Dot missing your vowels? Choose Your vowels, sing “ee”, “oo” and “ah”, and the chart follows your voice from then on.
