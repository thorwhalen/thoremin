funfon

Games that listen. Everything runs in your browser: the microphone is heard on this device and nothing is sent anywhere. Headphones help, so the microphone does not hear the game's own sounds.

Sing

Sing AlongA melody scrolls past: sing each note as it passes, scored by how long you stay in tune. Match the NoteA note plays: find it with your voice and hold it in tune until the ring fills. Pop the NotesYour pitch moves a dot across, your loudness moves it up: hold it on a balloon until it bursts. Pitch MazeSing the ball through each wall's opening, then stay on the note's lane down a road to the horizon.

Rhythm

Play Along LessonsClap, tap or play each note as it reaches the line: perfect, great or good, early or late.

Speech

VowelsThe vowel chart, playable: touch it to hear a vowel, say one to see where yours sits, then pop vowel balloons.

Make

Sound mapperTeach it a few taps (a table, a pot), give each a drum, and every tap plays its drum straight away.

Set up

Audio timingTap along to clicks for half a minute, so the timed games know your device's delay.
