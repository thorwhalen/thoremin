Instruments

Every instrument that comes with thoremin, grouped by what you play it with. Open one for what to do, and how it works.

Field instruments

Play the air in front of the camera like a theremin: your hands choose the pitch and the loudness, and each instrument changes what the fingers, the face and the open hand do.

Air instruments

Mime an instrument and it plays: strike a drum, pluck a bass, strum a guitar or blow a flute at nothing.

Chords and harmony

Point at chords instead of notes: a map or a wheel of a key's chords, a keyboard or a band that sets the chords for you to play over, a score whose chords you conduct.

Sign instruments

Fingerspell American Sign Language to the camera and see the sign, or hear a chord for each letter from A to G.

Drawn instruments

Draw shapes in the air with a pinch and play them: closed shapes become drums, open ones strings.

Listening games

Games and lessons that listen to the microphone: sing a melody and watch your voice on the notes, clap or play along a note highway, find a note and hold it. They measure your timing first, so the judging is fair on your device.
