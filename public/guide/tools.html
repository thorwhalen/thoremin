Tools

What you use ON an instrument: measuring, teaching it your own faces, recording, conducting. Open the Tools menu in the app to reach them.

  • Annotations

    Press Annotations at the bottom right and switch Annotation mode on, before you record.

  • Assistant

    Open Tools, then Assistant.

  • Commands

    Press Cmd or Ctrl and K.

  • Conductor

    Open Tools, then Conductor, and press Start conducting.

  • Your faces

    Open Tools, then Your faces, then Record my faces.

  • Gestures

    Open Tools, then Gestures.

  • Your hand shapes

    Open Tools, then Your hand shapes, then Record my hand shapes.

  • Feature Lab

    Open Tools, then Feature Lab, and press Start measuring.

  • Patcher

    Open Tools, then Patcher.

  • Your poses

    Open Tools, then Your poses, then Record my poses.

  • Record

    Press Tap to play, then Record at the bottom right.

  • Replay

    Record a take with Record, ticking Feature stream (JSONL).

  • Song

    Open Tools, then Song, and load a song file.

  • Trainer

    Open Tools, then Trainer.

Thoremin turns your camera into a musical instrument. Everything runs in your browser: nothing you play is uploaded. Open the app, or the home page for the listening games.
