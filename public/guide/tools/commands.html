Tools

Commands

What to do

  1. Press Cmd or Ctrl and K.
  2. Type the name of any setting.
  3. Press Enter to set it.

The fastest way to change anything: every setting is a searchable command. An option setting gives you a dropdown; a number gives you a small form with the real bounds. It works while playing.

Keyboard shortcuts
Key Action
Up / Down Octave up / down
Right / Left Magnetism up / down
m Mute everything
t Open the Tools list
Cmd/Ctrl + Z Undo the last setting change
Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + Z Redo
1 to 9 Toggle annotation 1 to 9, while annotation mode is on
Cmd/Ctrl + K Command palette

Shortcuts never fire while you are typing in a text field.

Thoremin turns your camera into a musical instrument. Everything runs in your browser: nothing you play is uploaded. Open the app, or the home page for the listening games.
