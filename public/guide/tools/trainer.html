Tools

Trainer

What to do

  1. Open Tools, then Trainer.
  2. Start the one-minute guided take and follow the cues.
  3. Pick how many categories it should find.

A guided take: it asks you to look left, smile, open your mouth and so on, and learns from what you do. Your faces is the newer, easier way to teach faces; the Trainer is where cues and routines live.

Where takes go

In a browser with a folder picker (Chrome, Edge), press Save takes to a folder... under the Start button and every take is written straight into it. Safari and Firefox have no folder picker, and take a zip instead.

Thoremin turns your camera into a musical instrument. Everything runs in your browser: nothing you play is uploaded. Open the app.
