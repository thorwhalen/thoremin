Tools

Your poses

What to do

  1. Open Tools, then Your poses, then Record my poses.
  2. Do the three short demonstrations (closer and further, a step aside, a slight turn), then each pose, held for a moment.
  3. Sort and name the groups. The conductor can use them to start, hold and stop.

The same tool as Your faces, for your body. It needs most of your body in view, otherwise it says "Cannot see you".

Left and right

Tick Left and right are the same pose before you record if one arm up should be one pose, whichever arm.

Use them

A pose model can start, hold and cut off the Conductor. In the editor, the Pose categorizer section chooses which model an instrument plays.

Its settings: Pose categorizer.
