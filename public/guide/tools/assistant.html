Tools

Assistant

What to do

  1. Open Tools, then Assistant.
  2. Paste an API key for one provider (it stays in your browser).
  3. Say what you want: "make the left hand an octave lower".

A chat that operates the instrument for you. It uses the same commands as the palette and the keyboard, so it can only do things you could have done yourself.

Your key

It is bring-your-own-key: you paste a key for one provider (OpenAI, Anthropic or Google). The key is stored in your browser and sent only to that provider; there is no thoremin server in the middle. Anything that could lose your work, such as saving over an instrument, stops and asks you first.

Thoremin turns your camera into a musical instrument. Everything runs in your browser: nothing you play is uploaded. Open the app, or the home page for the listening games.
