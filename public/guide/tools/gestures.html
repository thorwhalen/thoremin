Tools

Gestures

What to do

  1. Open Tools, then Gestures.
  2. Choose a command for a hand pose: fist, open palm or pinch.
  3. Make the pose: the command runs, hands-free.

Run commands without touching the screen. Bind a hand pose, or any group you taught in Your faces, Your hand shapes or Your poses, to a command such as mute, octave up or load an instrument.

Your categories

Your categories binds each group of a saved model to a command: entering the group fires the command. Commands that could lose your work (saving over an instrument) are not allowed here.

Thoremin turns your camera into a musical instrument. Everything runs in your browser: nothing you play is uploaded. Open the app.
