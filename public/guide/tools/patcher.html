Tools

Patcher

What to do

  1. Open Tools, then Patcher.
  2. Click a box to see what flows through it.
  3. Change a setting in the box's panel and hear it at once.

A map of the instrument as it is wired right now: every part that reads the camera, works out your hands and face, chooses notes and makes the sound, as a box, with lines for what flows between them. Switch instruments and the map changes with it.

How it works

Each box is one part of the instrument; the lines show where its numbers come from and where they go, left to right. A dashed line carries the value from the moment before (a loop back). Clicking a box shows the numbers it is sending right now and the settings that feed it, the same settings as in the instrument editor: change one here and it changes there, and Undo takes it back. Drag the boxes, scroll to zoom. You cannot rewire the instrument here yet: the map shows the wiring, it does not change it.

Thoremin turns your camera into a musical instrument. Everything runs in your browser: nothing you play is uploaded. Open the app.
