Tools

Feature Lab

What to do

  1. Open Tools, then Feature Lab, and press Start measuring.
  2. Open Groups and pick which groups of features to show.
  3. Move your face or hands and watch the meters.

A measuring instrument, not a sound: what can you actually control? A live grid of meters over more than 300 features of the face, hands and body.

How it works

Every feature is normalized as you move, so a blendshape (0 to 1), a finger curl and a head angle all read as comparable levels. Each feature has a hint: easy, moderate or involuntary. You can write your own formula over any features and it becomes another meter. Save named views and load them back.

Thoremin turns your camera into a musical instrument. Everything runs in your browser: nothing you play is uploaded. Open the app.
