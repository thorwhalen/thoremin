Sign Wheel

What to do

  1. Fingerspell a letter from A to G and hold it a moment.
  2. The wheel turns to that letter's chord in the key, and its wedges show the chords one move away.
  3. Spell the next letter to go on; your hand does not need to rest in between.

The chord wheel, steered by your letters instead of your pushes. In D major, F goes to F-sharp minor and C to C-sharp diminished, because the key's accidentals apply. Watching the wedges as you spell shows which move each letter was from the last chord: a fourth up, a third down.

How it works

The sign reader names your letter. A letter held for a few readings goes to its chord, and the chords are voice-led, so each one moves as little as it can from the one before. Signs that are not A to G change nothing. The key and how the chord sounds are in Sound; how long a letter must be held is in Chord wheel. Spelling needs Sign language on, which this instrument turns on.

Its settings: Chord wheel, Sign language, Sound. Every knob: the manual.
