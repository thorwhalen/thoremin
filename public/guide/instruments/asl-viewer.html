ASL Viewer

What to do

  1. Allow the camera and hold a hand up.
  2. Fingerspell a letter or sign a word.
  3. The sign is written large on the video, with a bar for every sign it knows.

See what the camera reads: the American Sign Language alphabet and a few common words (yes, no, hello, thank you, please, bye). The moving letters J and Z and the word sorry are not read reliably yet: the reader is trained only on openly licensed recordings, and too few of those show them.

How it works

A small recogniser reads the shape of your hand. It downloads only when you first open an ASL instrument, so the rest of the app never pays for it.

Its settings: Sign language. Every knob: the manual.
