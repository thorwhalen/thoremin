Conduct the Chords

What to do

  1. Open the Conductor tool: a Haydn quartet is loaded.
  2. Beat time in front of the camera.
  3. The chords of the piece play on your beat, under the first violin's tune.

Conduct a chord chart: the chords play voice-led and arpeggiated on your beat.
