Sign Map

What to do

  1. Fingerspell a letter from A to G with your higher hand: its chord lights up on the map.
  2. Raise or lower your right hand to play a melody on that chord's notes.
  3. The chord stays while you play; spell another letter to change it.

One hand chooses the chord by spelling it, the other plays over it. In D major, F lights F-sharp minor and C lights C-sharp diminished.

How it works

The sign reader names your letter. A letter read for a fifth of a second lights its chord on the chord map, and the chord holds while your hand passes through other shapes. The melody hand's height picks a note from the chord, and tilting it changes the set (chord tones, pentatonic, the scale). Your right hand plays the melody; change that with Chord hand in Chord map. The key is in Sound; how the chord sounds is in Face, once you set the face to chord.

Its settings: Chord map, Sign language, Sound, Face. Every knob: the manual.
