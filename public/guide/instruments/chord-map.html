Chord Map

What to do

  1. Hold your left hand over a chord on the map to hold that chord.
  2. Move your right hand up and down to play a melody on the chord's notes.
  3. Move the left hand to another chord: the change is smooth.

A key's seven chords laid out as a map on the video. The left hand points at a chord; the right hand plays a melody over it.
