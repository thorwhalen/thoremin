Face Wheel

What to do

  1. Look at the camera with a calm face.
  2. Hold an expression for a moment: a smile moves to one chord, a sad face to another.
  3. Each expression you hold is a move on the wheel.

The chord wheel steered by your face instead of your hand.

How it works

Each expression stands for one of the wheel's moves: for example a smile goes to the dominant and a sad face to the relative minor. Hold the expression for a short moment and the move is made, then relax before the next one. Your hands are free, so you can play the melody with them at the same time.

Settings

In Chord wheel: what steers it (your hand, your face, or both) and how long an expression must be held. The face model itself is set up in Face.

Its settings: Chord wheel, Face. Every knob: the manual.
