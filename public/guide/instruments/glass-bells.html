Glass Bells

What to do

  1. Look at the camera and change your expression: smile, open your mouth, raise your brows.
  2. Each expression plays a chord that rings as a shimmering arpeggio.
  3. You can play with a hand at the same time.

Your face plays chords: the expression decides which chord of the key sounds, and the chord is played as a rising arpeggio.

How it works

In Face, the mapping Expression → chord turns facial expressions into the chords of the key (a smile, a frown, a surprised face each pick a different one). The rendering Arpeggio ↑ plays the chord's notes one after another at a steady tempo. Your own faces can replace the built-in ones: see Your faces.

Its settings: Face, Sound. Every knob: the manual.
