Finger Cues

What to do

  1. Hold a hand up so all five fingers show.
  2. Watch the lines and the bars on the video: they show each finger's value.
  3. Curl a finger to see its bar move and hear its effect.

Finger FX with every aid switched on, to learn how the fingers read.

How it works

All fingers are routed to effects, and the video shows both a line from each fingertip to the thumb (with its value and effect) and a bar graph. The lines and bars are overlay elements: switch them on or off in Overlay.

Its settings: Hand, Overlay. Every knob: the manual.
