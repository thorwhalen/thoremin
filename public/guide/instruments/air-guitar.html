Air Guitar

What to do

  1. First teach it your chord shapes (the editor's Air guitar section).
  2. Make a chord shape with one hand.
  3. Strum with the other: it plays that chord on six strings.

You decide what a chord shape looks like. It learns your own hand shapes, then plays the right chord when you strum.

How it works

The chord shapes you enrol are the ones it recognises, so you can use any shapes you like. A strum is predicted before the frame that shows it. A hand-shape model you carved with Your hand shapes can be the chords.

Its settings: Air guitar. Every knob: the manual.
