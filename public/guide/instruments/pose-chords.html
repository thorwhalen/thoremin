Pose Chords

What to do

  1. Stand back so the camera sees your whole body.
  2. Hold a pose a moment: both arms up, the left arm out, the right arm out, or a crouch.
  3. Each pose plays its own chord of the key; standing at rest plays none.

Four poses, four chords. It plays a shipped example of a pose categorizer, made from the app's own scripted body, so it may not read you well: teach it your own poses in Your poses and choose them in Categorizer chords.

How it works

The body camera reads your pose, and the nearest of the four poses the example learned, held a moment, chooses the chord. The first pose plays the key's home chord, then the fourth, the fifth and the sixth; each can be changed in Categorizer chords. It needs the body camera, so it starts a little slower than a hands-only instrument.

Its settings: Categorizer chords, Sound. Every knob: the manual.
