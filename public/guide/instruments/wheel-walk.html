Wheel Walk

What to do

  1. Hold your left hand on a chord.
  2. Push toward a move and keep holding it out.
  3. The wheel keeps making that move, one per beat: up walks the cycle of fourths.

Walk a chain of chords on the beat by holding a push.

How it works

Instead of one change per push, a push held out keeps making the same move on every beat. Pushing toward the fourth walks all seven chords of the key and comes home. The chords are arpeggiated so you can hear the beat, and the beat is the Chord sound's tempo (or a song you loaded). Bring your hand back to the middle to stop.

Settings

In Chord wheel: walking on the beat, and every how many beats a move is made. The tempo and the arpeggio pattern are in Sound.

Its settings: Chord wheel, Sound. Every knob: the manual.
