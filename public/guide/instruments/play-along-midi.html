Play Along (MIDI)

What to do

  1. Connect a MIDI keyboard (in Chrome or Edge) and play chords on it.
  2. The chord you hold lights up on the map.
  3. Play a melody over it with your hand.

A MIDI keyboard chooses the chords; your hand plays the melody over them.

How it works

Hold a chord on the keyboard, in any inversion, and after a very short pause it lights up on the map, as if your hand were pointing at it. Your other hand plays the melody on that chord, or strums it if the map is set to strum. The app listens to MIDI keyboards only while a map follows MIDI, and the browser asks your permission the first time. Safari has no MIDI support: use Chrome or Edge.

Settings

In Chord map: what chooses the chords (your hand, a MIDI keyboard, or the microphone), and how long a held chord must be steady before it counts.

Its settings: Chord map. Every knob: the manual.
