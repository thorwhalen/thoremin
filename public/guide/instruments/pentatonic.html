Pentatonic

What to do

  1. Allow the camera, then tap anywhere to start the sound.
  2. Hold a hand up: left and right is pitch, up and down is loudness.
  3. Raise a second hand for a second voice.

The gentle default. The index fingertip is the note, and the notes are snapped toward a five-note scale, so it is hard to play a wrong one.

How it works

Your hand's position is read as a note of the scale (the pentatonic scale in C by default). Magnetism decides how hard the pitch snaps to the scale's notes: 0 % glides freely, 100 % jumps from note to note. Each hand is its own voice with its own scale, sound and range.

Make it yours

In the editor, Sound sets the root note, the scale, the octave range and the sound of each voice; Hand sets the magnetism and what the open hand and the pinch do. Save the result as your own instrument.

Its settings: Sound, Hand. Every knob: the manual.
