Pulse Organ

What to do

  1. Look at the camera and change your expression.
  2. Each expression plays a chord that pulses like an organ.
  3. Play a hand on top.

The same face-chord idea, but the chord is re-struck in a steady rhythm instead of arpeggiated.

How it works

The rendering pulse repeats the chord at a tempo. Everything else works as in Glass Bells.

Its settings: Face, Sound. Every knob: the manual.
