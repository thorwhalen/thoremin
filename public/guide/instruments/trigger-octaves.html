Trigger Octaves

What to do

  1. Play notes with your hand.
  2. Pinch the index finger for a jump of one octave up.
  3. Pinch the middle finger and hold it to let the sound through; let go and it stops.

Two discrete triggers instead of continuous ones: a pinch is a switch.

How it works

Fingers can act as continuous controls or as triggers that fire once when you cross a threshold. Here the index trigger shifts the octave and the middle trigger opens the gate (no pinch, no sound).

Its settings: Hand. Every knob: the manual.
