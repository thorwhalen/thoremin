Conduct and dance

Your whole body can lead the music. Open the Conductor and the Song tools to try these; each page there starts with what to do.

Conduct a score

Load a MIDI or MusicXML score (a demo is built in), press Start conducting and beat time in front of the camera. A sampled orchestra plays and follows your tempo and your loudness. You choose which parts play, and the orchestra is optional: tick Play with the orchestra and it downloads the first time.

Hand or body

By default your beating hand's strokes are the beats. Choose body and several joints (arm and trunk) are read: start, hold, fermata and cut-off come from your body, and your other hand can shape loudness and staccato. Learn my timing measures how far each joint lags a click and keeps the ones it measured clearly, so the beat lands when you meant it. Body conducting needs most of your upper body in view.

Start, hold and stop with poses

A pose model you taught in Your poses can start, hold and cut off the conductor: raise your arm and keep still to get ready, then give the upbeat. See Your poses for how to teach "ready", "hold" and "cut off".

Dance the orchestra

Choose Dance the orchestra and the score follows a dancer instead of a conductor: the tempo follows your stride, a written fermata holds for one cycle of yours, and the piece loops while you keep dancing. Turn body tracking on in the editor's Body section first, or let the conductor ask for it.

A song that follows you

In the Song tool, load a song file. Its beat is tracked in your browser. Tick Follow the dancer and the song's tempo follows your pulse within a range you set (10 percent by default), whether you bounce on every beat or every other. Start on your step lands a beat of the song on your step when you press Play. Pausing the song keeps the lock.

Conduct a recording

The song itself can follow your conducting: you choose which beat of the song your first beat lands on, and on a hold the song can fade and slow down instead of cutting. Both are off until you set them.

Conduct the chords

Conduct the Chords plays the chords of a piece, voice-led and arpeggiated, on your beat.

Thoremin turns your camera into a musical instrument. Everything runs in your browser: nothing you play is uploaded. Open the app.
