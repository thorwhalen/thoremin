Instruments

Listening games

Games and lessons that listen to the microphone: sing a melody and watch your voice on the notes, clap or play along a note highway, find a note and hold it. They measure your timing first, so the judging is fair on your device.

  • Match the Note

    Allow the microphone and press Start.

  • Play Along Lessons

    Allow the microphone, choose a lesson and press Start.

  • Sing Along

    Allow the microphone, choose a lesson in the Listening games section and press Start.

Thoremin turns your camera into a musical instrument. Everything runs in your browser: nothing you play is uploaded. Open the app, or the home page for the listening games.
